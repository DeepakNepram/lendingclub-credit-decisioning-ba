# Interview Guide — Head of Credit (Sponsor)

> Scenario is hypothetical; all data is real LendingClub public data (2007–2018).

**Purpose:** Understand the sponsor's biggest portfolio-insight gaps, how they define key risk
metrics, and what would make them trust and adopt a single governed dashboard.
**Format:** ~15 minutes, semi-structured. **Interviewer:** Deepak Nepram (BA).
**Persona:** Owns credit risk and portfolio quality; answers to the board; pain is that risk
concentrations surface late and numbers disagree between teams.

## Questions
1. **Which portfolio question takes you longest to answer today, and why?**
   *Listening for:* the highest-value view to build first; where the manual effort hurts most.
2. **Which borrower segments worry you most, and on what evidence?**
   *Listening for:* candidate risk dimensions (grade, term, purpose, vintage) and whether the
   concern is data-backed or anecdotal — this shapes the risk tab and the rules catalog.
3. **How do you define charge-off rate today — over all issued loans, or resolved loans only?**
   *Listening for:* the governed denominator. This single definition decision drives the KPI
   dictionary and every charge-off number on the dashboard.
4. **What would make you trust a number on a dashboard enough to take it to the board?**
   *Listening for:* the bar for release — documented definitions, independent cross-checks,
   reproducibility.
5. **What should trigger manual review of an application?**
   *Listening for:* the seed rules (DTI, grade, term) and how strict the sponsor wants to be —
   note the trade-off against operational capacity for the Ops interview.
6. **What does success look like 90 days after release?**
   *Listening for:* adoption and decision-speed measures to put in the charter and PRD.

## Close
Confirm the charge-off definition, summarize the segments of concern, and state the next step
(quantify charge-off by segment and size the implied review queue). Thank the interviewee.
